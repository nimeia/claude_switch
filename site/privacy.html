Claude Switch

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Claude Switch is a Windows tray application that switches between Claude Code accounts on your own computer. It has no server, no account of its own, and no sign-up. Everything it manages stays on your machine.

The short version: the app reads and writes your local Claude Code configuration so it can switch accounts, and it talks to Anthropic's API with your own credentials to read your usage. It collects no analytics, sends nothing to the authors, and has no telemetry of any kind.

What the app reads and writes on your computer

To switch accounts without restarting Claude Code, the app manages the files Claude Code itself uses:

  • %USERPROFILE%\.claude.json and its backup and lock files — the active account and Claude Code settings
  • %USERPROFILE%\.claude\ — including .credentials.json, settings.json, settings.local.json, and the projects and sessions folders that the app reads to list your recent conversations
  • Windows Credential Manager entries that Claude Code stores tokens in
  • Its own settings file, holding the accounts you added, their labels, and your preferences

These files contain personal information: the email address of each Claude account you add, OAuth tokens, and the names and paths of the projects you have worked on. The app reads them to do its job and writes them back when you switch. It does not copy them anywhere else and does not transmit them to the authors or to any third party.

Network connections the app makes

  • Anthropic (api.anthropic.com, platform.claude.com) — using your own account credentials, to sign in and to read the usage figures shown in the 5-hour and 7-day meters. Your use of those services is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy.
  • IP geolocation services (ipwho.is, ipapi.co, ip-api.com) — only when you use the proxy and region feature, to show which country a proxy exits from. These requests disclose the exit IP address to the service that answers, which is the point of the check. No account information is sent.
  • GitHub — to check for a newer release, when you ask it to.

If you configure a proxy in the app, these requests go through it.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage statistics
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No account, server, or database belonging to this project — there is nowhere for your data to be sent
  • Nothing is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties

Deleting your data

Removing an account in the app removes its stored entry. Uninstalling the app removes the app and its own settings; your Claude Code configuration in %USERPROFILE%\.claude is left alone, because it belongs to Claude Code rather than to this app. You can delete it yourself at any time.

Children

Claude Switch is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date above.

Contact
